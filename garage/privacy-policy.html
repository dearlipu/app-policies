The short version

  • Your tasks stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.
  • Payments go through Google Play. We never see your card details.

This summary is only a guide. The numbered sections below are the actual policy.

What Garage stores

Garage keeps the things you enter into it:

  • task names, notes and whether they’re done;
  • categories, with their colours and order;
  • reminder dates, times and repeat settings;
  • widget and display settings, and other app preferences.

All of this lives in Garage’s private storage on your device. Garage has no server, so none of it is sent to us.

Garage doesn’t ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and there is no account to create.

What we do with it

The data is used only to run the app: showing and editing your tasks, drawing the widget, remembering your settings, and making backups or restoring them when you ask.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone using Android’s own alarm and notification system. They don’t go through any push-notification server.

Because your data never reaches us, we can’t see it, sell it, or hand it to anyone.

Backup and restore

If you tap Backup Data, Garage creates a .grg file with your tasks, categories and reminders. You choose where it’s saved.

Garage does not encrypt this file. Anyone who gets a copy can read it, so don’t leave it in a shared folder or send it to people you don’t trust.

Restore Data replaces the data currently in the app with what’s in the file you pick. Save a copy of your current data first if you might want it.

Separately, Android may include Garage’s data in its own device backup, depending on your phone, its manufacturer and your backup settings. Android and your backup provider (usually Google) manage those copies, not us.

Sharing

Garage can open Android’s share sheet so you can send task text to another app. This only happens when you start it and pick where it goes.

Once you share something, the app you chose handles it under its own privacy policy. Garage doesn’t send your tasks to anyone on its own.

Third parties

Garage doesn’t include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. If that ever changes, we’ll update this policy before the change ships and say what those tools collect.

Android itself processes some information while Garage works, for example when it delivers a notification, lets you pick a file, or runs a device backup.

Paid features, if you buy them, are handled by Google Play Billing under Google’s terms and privacy policy. We don’t receive your card or bank details. Google tells us only that a purchase was completed.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. You can do that by:

  • deleting individual tasks or categories in Garage;
  • clearing Garage’s storage in Android settings;
  • uninstalling Garage;
  • deleting any backup files you saved.

Copies outside the app, such as Android device backups and anything you shared with other apps, are kept or deleted according to those services’ own settings.

Security

Garage keeps its data in Android’s app-private storage, which other apps can’t normally read. No system is perfectly secure, though.

Use a screen lock on your phone and keep your backup files somewhere safe.

Children

Garage has no accounts and sends nothing to our servers, so we don’t collect personal information from anyone, children included. A parent or guardian should still keep an eye on what a child does with their device and with any other apps or services they use.

Your choices and rights

You decide what goes into Garage, and you can change or delete it at any time. Notifications, reminders, backups and sharing can all be controlled in the app and in Android settings.

If you have a question or request about privacy, email us. We’ll answer in good faith, though we can only help with data we actually hold, and for Garage that is very little because your data is on your phone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Garage changes or the law does. The current version is always on this page, and the “Effective” date at the top shows when it last changed.

If a change affects how your data is handled, we’ll make it clear in the app or on the Play Store listing.
