Privacy Policy

App: Garage • Last updated: October 11, 2026

Garage is a task and reminder app. This policy describes what information the app handles and how it is used. Garage does not require an account, and the app does not send your task information to Garage-operated servers.

1. Information the App Handles

Garage stores information you enter so its features can work, including:

  • Task and category names, task status, category colors and ordering
  • Reminder times and repeat settings
  • App and widget preferences, such as appearance and task display settings

This information is stored locally on your device. Garage does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, advertising identifier or usage analytics.

2. How Information Is Used

Your information is used on your device to display and organize tasks, configure the widget, and schedule reminders. If you allow notifications, Android uses local alarm and notification features to deliver those reminders. Garage does not use a remote push-notification service.

3. Backups and Sharing

If you choose Backup Data, Garage creates a .grg backup containing your categories, tasks and reminders. You choose where to save it. The backup contains readable task data and is not encrypted by Garage, so store it somewhere you consider secure.

If you choose Restore Data, Garage reads the file you select and replaces the existing categories, tasks and reminders.

Garage can also share task text using Android’s share interface. Sharing happens only when you choose to do so. The app you select will handle the information according to its own privacy policy.

Android device backup may include Garage app data, depending on your device and backup settings. Android and your device’s backup provider manage that process.

4. Third-Party Services

Garage does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not send your task data to a Garage-operated service. Android system features may handle information when you schedule reminders, save or select a backup file, share task text, or use device backup.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your tasks, reminders and preferences remain on your device until you delete them, clear Garage’s app data or uninstall the app. You can delete exported backup files from the location where you saved them. Android-managed backups are subject to your device and backup provider’s settings.

6. Data Security

Garage stores its data using Android’s app storage. Access to that data is subject to your device’s security. Exported .grg backups are not encrypted by Garage; protect them as you would other files containing personal information.

7. Children’s Privacy

Garage does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account and does not send information to Garage-operated servers.

8. Your Choices

You can manage reminders and notifications through the app and Android settings. You can delete tasks in the app, clear the app’s data or uninstall Garage. You control where exported backups are saved and which app receives information you choose to share.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if Garage’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at the privacy-policy URL listed on the app’s Google Play page, along with its updated date.
