Privacy Policy

App: Garage
Last updated: November 7, 2026

Garage is a task management and reminder application designed to help you organize everyday activities and manage tasks through your Android device.

Your privacy matters. Garage is designed to store your tasks and preferences locally on your device. Garage does not require an account, and does not operate a remote server that receives your task content.

1. Information We Handle

Garage stores information you voluntarily enter, including:

  • Task names, descriptions and completion status.
  • Category names, colors and ordering.
  • Reminder dates, times and repeat settings.
  • Widget configurations and display preferences.
  • Other app settings and preferences.

This information is stored locally within the app's storage on your device.

Garage does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number or location to use its core features. Garage does not operate an account-registration system.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used to:

  • Create, display, edit and organize tasks.
  • Display task information in Android widgets.
  • Schedule and deliver local reminders.
  • Remember your app settings and preferences.
  • Perform backup and restore operations when requested.

Reminders use Android's local alarm and notification features. Garage does not use a remote push-notification server for its reminders.

3. Local Storage, Backup and Restore

Garage stores its application data in Android's app-specific storage.

If you choose the Backup Data feature, Garage creates a .grg backup file containing supported app data, such as tasks, categories and reminders.

You choose where to save the backup file. The exported backup is not encrypted by Garage. Anyone who obtains access to that file may be able to read its contents.

When you use Restore Data, Garage reads the backup file you select and replaces the corresponding existing app data. Make sure you have a separate backup before restoring if you want to preserve your current information.

Android device backup may include Garage app data depending on Android, device manufacturer and your backup settings. Such backups are managed by Android and the applicable backup provider.

4. Sharing Information

Garage may provide Android's sharing interface so that you can voluntarily share task text or other supported content.

Sharing happens only when you initiate the action and select another application or recipient. The selected application handles the information under its own privacy policy.

Garage does not independently send your task information to other people or services.

5. Third-Party Services

Garage's core task-management features do not require a Garage-operated cloud service or user account.

Android system components may process information when you schedule reminders, display notifications, select files, share content or use device backup.

If you purchase an optional premium feature, in-app product or subscription, the transaction is processed through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment processing under its applicable terms and privacy policy.

Garage does not receive your complete payment card or banking credentials through Google Play Billing.

6. Advertising and Analytics

Garage's current release does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, provided that no such SDKs are included in the published application.

If advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services are introduced in a future release, this policy will be updated to explain the information those services process and how they use it.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your tasks, reminders and preferences remain in the app's local storage until you delete them or remove the app data.

You can remove your information by:

  • Deleting individual tasks or categories in Garage.
  • Clearing Garage's app storage through Android settings.
  • Uninstalling Garage.
  • Deleting exported backup files from their saved location.

Android-managed backups and copies shared with other applications are subject to the respective providers' retention and deletion settings.

8. Data Security

Garage uses Android's app-specific storage to keep its application data separate from ordinary files belonging to other applications, subject to Android's security model.

No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and keep exported backup files in a secure location.

9. Children's Privacy

Garage does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account-registration system or a Garage-operated server.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of devices and any optional third-party services.

10. Your Choices and Rights

You control the tasks and information you enter into Garage. You can manage notifications, reminders, exported backups and sharing through the app and Android settings.

You may contact us with questions or requests relating to privacy and personal information using the contact details below.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Garage's features, services or data practices change.

The latest version will be published at the Privacy Policy URL listed on the app's Google Play page. The updated date will be shown at the top of this page.
